## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>What is Viral Suite?</b></summary>

Viral Suite seamlessly connects the entire workflow of the social media journey — from content creation to social media management to social media marketing, including paid ads and influencer marketing. We are the first in the world attempting to unify them and make them seamless.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What makes Viral Suite different from tools like Hootsuite or Later?</b></summary>

Tools like Hootsuite and Later focus primarily on scheduling and publishing. Viral Suite goes further by connecting the entire workflow from content planning and social media management through to paid ads and influencer marketing — everything lives and operates inside one connected system.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Viral Suite a social media management tool or an influencer marketing platform?</b></summary>

It is both, and more. Viral Suite is designed as a broader social media workflow platform — social media management and influencer marketing are connected parts of the same system, not separate products bolted together.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Who is Viral Suite built for?</b></summary>

Brands, agencies, and creators who need a more connected system for planning content, managing social media, running campaigns, and coordinating influencer activity — without the chaos of juggling multiple disconnected tools.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is Viral Suite available globally?</b></summary>

Yes. Viral Suite is built for global use. The influencer marketing platform launched initially in India and is expanding to additional countries, while self-serve access is designed to be available globally.
</details>

<summary><b>How can I request access or book a demo?</b></summary>

You can [request direct access](https://app.viralsuite.co/) to explore the product yourself, or [schedule a demo](https://calendar.app.google/8Gi1wTYmz2T5aWsY9) for a guided walkthrough of how Viral Suite fits your workflow.
</details>
